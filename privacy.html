Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

What the app does

improv-video is a personal desktop application that uploads videos recorded by its owner to the owner's YouTube channel using the YouTube API Services.

Data the app accesses

  • Video files on the owner's computer and camera memory card, processed locally.
  • Access to the owner's YouTube account with the youtube.upload scope, used only to upload videos and set their title, description, recording date and privacy status.

Storage and sharing

  • All processing happens on the owner's computer. No data is sent to the developer or any server other than Google's YouTube API.
  • The Google access token is stored locally in the operating system keychain.
  • The app does not collect analytics, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not read any other data from the Google account.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app removes the locally stored token.

YouTube and Google policies

By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
